Last updated: October 9, 2026

Vera View is a read-only viewer for drawing files. It works entirely on your device.

  • No data collection. Vera View does not collect, store, or share any personal information, usage data, analytics, or crash reports.
  • No network access. Vera View does not connect to the internet or to any server.
  • Your files stay on your device. Vera View reads only the files you choose to open, and only to display them. It never modifies, copies, or uploads them.
  • No accounts or tracking. There is no sign-in, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions about this policy: see the support page.
